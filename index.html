✩ OUR SERVICES ✩

Expert Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Services

Comprehensive physiotherapy treatments designed to relieve pain, restore mobility, improve strength, and support faster recovery.

  • Neurological Physiotherapy

    Specialized rehabilitation for stroke, paralysis, Parkinson's disease, and neurological disorders.

  • Orthopedic Physiotherapy

    Treatment for joint pain, arthritis, frozen shoulder, neck pain, back pain, and muscle injuries.

  • Geriatric Physiotherapy

    Helping senior citizens improve mobility, flexibility, balance, and overall physical function.

  • Pediatric Physiotherapy

    Therapy solutions for children with developmental delays, posture issues, and movement disorders.

  • Pain Management Therapy

    Advanced treatment for chronic pain, cervical pain, sciatica, shoulder pain, and knee pain.

  • Post Surgery Rehabilitation

    Recovery programs after knee replacement, hip replacement, ligament surgery, and spine surgery.

  • Post Fracture Physiotherapy

    Regain strength, flexibility, mobility, and confidence after fractures and orthopedic injuries.

  • Sports Injury Rehabilitation

    Customized rehabilitation programs for athletes and active individuals recovering from injuries.

  • Cardiac Rehab Physiotherapy

    Exercise-based rehabilitation programs designed to support heart health and recovery.

  • Home Physiotherapy

    Professional physiotherapy treatment delivered at home for comfort and convenience.

  • Respiratory Physiotherapy

    Breathing exercises and chest physiotherapy to improve respiratory function and lung health.

  • Posture Correction Therapy

    Improve body alignment, reduce strain, and prevent long-term posture-related problems.
